Apple Notes to Pages Converter

It’s surprisingly hard to move text from Apple Notes into Apple Pages without losing the formatting. This tool matches your chosen Pages font and size while keeping bold, underline, spacing, and indentation. I made it to turn notes for a book into the format I was writing in Pages.

Paste your note below → choose your Pages font in Font settings → save the RTF → open it in TextEdit → copy all → paste into Pages.
